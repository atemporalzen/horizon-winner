Horizon Winner is an original implementation informed by these projects and research:

- atemporalzen/horizon, commit fcfbdeb204677040cb974b6c4d0bbc34851e1b43.
- atemporalzen/horizon-lna, commit 2112f486b808d3089d1912253ec287b23938d114.
- NCC Group's Singularity of Origin, especially its hexadecimal hostname
  convention, DNS session model, and publicly documented defenses.
- Jorian Woltjer's navigation research as discussed in HackerNotes Ep. 193,
  September 24, 2026. See docs/research.md for links and evidence limits.

No Go/JavaScript source or compiled executable from Horizon or Singularity is
vendored here. Dependency license notices are recorded by Go/npm metadata and
